# EKNX Genesis Market — Post-Launch Legal & Regulatory Readiness Checklist

The EKNX Genesis Market is LIVE on-chain.

This checklist is an internal/public transparency record. Technical launch
must not be represented as proof that every legal or regulatory obligation
has been finally satisfied.

## Already available

- EKNX deployed on BNB Smart Chain
- Public contract address
- 100,000,000 EKNX maximum supply
- 1,000,000 EKNX Market reserve
- 99,000,000 EKNX mining allocation/capacity model
- Public mining infrastructure
- Official Eureka 2026 Miner
- Automatic EKNX payout infrastructure
- Public risk disclosure
- Copyright / ownership notice

## Post-launch items requiring formal confirmation or evidence

- Legal identity of the offeror / relevant entity
- Registered address where legally required
- Tax identification / registration information where legally required
- Final EKNX legal classification
- Final curve parameters
- Final liquidity model
- Final use-of-funds policy if funds are raised
- Final conflicts-of-interest disclosure
- Applicable MiCA analysis
- Crypto-asset white paper if legally required
- Notification/publication process if legally required
- Marketing wording aligned with final legal documentation
- Consumer rights analysis
- Privacy / cookie review for launch infrastructure
- AML/KYC analysis where applicable
- Terms specific to the Market
- Jurisdiction and geographic restrictions if required

## Rule

The Genesis Market is already active.

Any unresolved legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, consumer-rights,
privacy or jurisdiction item must remain visible until formally resolved.

Future DEX migration, additional payment assets, new jurisdictions or
material changes to the sale architecture must not be represented as
active until their applicable technical, security and legal gates are
completed.
