01 / Digital intelligence

Meet Eureka. Then make it yours.

Our vision is a personal AI with shared foundational knowledge and a unique personality, memory and experiences for every person. The software is under development; the capabilities below describe the intended product, not features already launched.

Shared foundational intelligencePrivate individual memoryHuman permissions

One Eureka per person. More than an account in a chatbot.

Your Eureka would not start from nothing: it would use shared general knowledge and capabilities, then build a unique relationship through the interactions, choices and goals you authorize.

01

Face, body and voice

A customizable avatar: appearance, voice, expressions and visual presence — initially digital, with future animation possibilities.

02

Consistent personality

Communication style, operational values, preferences and boundaries shaped by the person within safety requirements.

03

Relationship memory

Long-lived context for projects, conversations and important decisions, with review, correction and deletion controls.

04

Learns to understand you

Progressive personalization without disclosing what you shared privately to other users.

05

User-defined goals

Projects, routines and plans Eureka could follow across sessions and devices.

06

Controls and trust

Approval for sensitive actions, auditable history and separation of personal memory from common knowledge.

MULTIMODAL / FUTURE

Talk. See. Hear. Understand the context.

Imagine saying “Eureka, look at what I am doing” and, after granting camera or screen access, receiving visual guidance. Or speaking naturally and continuing the same task in chat.

Camera, microphone and screen access must be optional, explicit and visible. We do not intend an AI that watches people without consent.

Not just answers. Real work.

The ambition is for Eureka to help solve practical problems and perform work through tools and integrations, with authorized access and confirmation of meaningful actions.

01

Organize life and work

Calendar, documents, reminders, projects and priorities once integrations are available.

02

Create and research

Research, writing, coding, file analysis and structured planning with verifiable references.

03

Coordinate agents

Specialized modules collaborating on subtasks, reporting results and requesting approval when needed.

An identity with continuity

A persistent digital personality — not a claim of consciousness.

Here, “a life of its own” describes continuity: remembering, learning, taking authorized initiative and adapting over time. We do not claim Eureka is conscious or biologically alive, or that these capabilities are publicly available today.

Straight answers

Essential questions

Will each user have their own Eureka?

That is the goal: shared foundational capabilities with individual identity, memory and preferences.

Will Eureka access my camera or microphone?

Only if that feature is released and you grant access. We plan clear access controls and active-use indicators.

Can I ask Eureka to perform full tasks?

It is planned, depending on available tools, safeguards and confirmation of required actions.

Will it always be learning?

The intent is improvement through controlled memory, learning and evaluation cycles, not uncontrolled model changes after every message.
