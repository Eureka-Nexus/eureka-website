02 / The Eureka universe

The Eureka World. A place for our digital presence.

We envision an active digital environment where your identity and Eureka inhabit personalized spaces, connect with tools and collaborate with other people and agents when you choose. This is a development vision, not an already available metaverse.

Personal spacesSeparated identitiesOpt-in collaboration

More than a chat. A connected world.

The digital world is envisioned as a visual and functional extension of the relationship among users, assistants, projects, communities and tools.

01

Your own space

A customizable private environment with projects, visual elements, files and tools you authorize.

02

Your Eureka’s presence

The digital avatar and personality accompany you across spaces while retaining relevant context.

03

Meet and collaborate

Voluntary connections between people and their Eurekas for projects, learning and shared work.

04

An agent team

Specialized agents that can work together with distinct roles and permissions.

05

Multimodal environments

Screen, voice, images and possible future 3D representations after technical validation.

06

Privacy by choice

Private by default; sharing and access require explicit consent, revocable where feasible.

IDENTITY / CONNECTIONS

One connected world. Every identity different.

The shared Eureka foundation supplies knowledge and capabilities. The personal experience — memories, preferences, projects and identity — belongs to each user. Connecting with someone does not mean merging private memories.

In the future, users could authorize collaboration among their digital identities while controlling what is shared and for how long.

CONCEPT VISUALIZATION · NOT RELEASED SOFTWARE

Connection with trust and boundaries.

A useful digital world needs both connection and protection. These are principles for architecture prior to launch.

01

Explicit consent

People control whether their Eureka can connect to others, devices, files or agents.

02

Separated memories

Collaboration does not imply access to personal history or cross-learning from private data.

03

Auditable actions

Meaningful tasks and information transfers should be traceable and reviewable.

A world yet to be built

The ambition is big. Delivery will be gradual.

We begin with intelligence, memory, identity and practical applications. Shared spaces will follow only after establishing security, utility and user control.
