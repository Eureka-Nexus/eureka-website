Shared intelligence. Unique identities.

Eureka starts from shared foundational knowledge and capabilities, but each user will be able to shape their own digital presence. Its personality, context, memories and goals develop through the relationship — without mixing personal data across users.

01

Talk naturally

Chat and, eventually, voice conversation with context, continuity and personalization.

02

See and hear, with permission

Camera, screen and microphone interaction only with permission and explicit controls.

03

Take action and get things done

Plan, research, create, organize and use tools with appropriate approval.

04

Shape a digital identity

Choose appearance, voice, style, goals and boundaries for a Eureka that learns your preferences.

05

Inhabit a digital world

Personal digital spaces and a community where people and agents can connect by their owners’ choice.

06

Grow with evidence

Learning, memory and new capabilities tested and evaluated before release.
