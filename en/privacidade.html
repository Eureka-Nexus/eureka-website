Information / Data protection

Privacy and user control.

Information about data processing on the current informational website. A dedicated policy will be prepared before launching public accounts, applications and multimodal features.

Current website information

01

Who to contact

Eureka Nexus receives privacy enquiries at official@eurekanexus.pt. Formal controller identification and required details will be reviewed with appropriate advice before activating public products.

02

Data collected by this website

This static site does not offer registration, payments or forms that submit data to a project server. When you contact us by email, you voluntarily send the data within that message.

03

Technical and hosting data

The hosting provider and security systems may process technical logs such as IP addresses and HTTP requests. Details, retention and applicable rights should be confirmed with the provider and in the final policy.

04

Cookies and external components

This version does not integrate project-configured advertising or analytics. Social links open external services governed by their own policies.

05

Your rights and requests

You may contact the project regarding personal data you sent and request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

06

Future AI features

Planned memory, camera, voice and tool features will require separate privacy assessment, consent controls and clear information before any public use.
