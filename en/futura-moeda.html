06 / Future network economics

A future token to reward useful work.

We are exploring an economic framework for verified computing, human learning contributions and future use of Eureka services. This proposal has not launched, is not for sale and may change.

Draft under reviewNo official price or dateNo active sale
UTILITY / VALIDATED CONTRIBUTION

Utility first. Economics second.

The goal is not to pay devices merely for being online. Work must produce genuine accepted outputs for the Eureka Compute Network. The final public model depends on technical tests, sustainability and legal review.

01

Verified computing

Distributed AI tasks that are completed and verified may create contribution units.

02

Human teaching and quality

Human contributions of knowledge, evaluation and curation may be recognized when approved.

03

Access to services

We envision internal service credits for the future application, avoiding blockchain transactions for every message.

Issuance and allocation rules

01
Period-based budget

A fixed period budget rather than rewards scaling with the number of connected devices.

02
Validated quality

Distribution based on accepted work weight, with verification and anti-fraud rules.

03
Controlled settlement

Internally recorded credits and possible batched payouts, subject to future thresholds and operating costs.

04
Governance and audit

Final contracts, network, rules and safeguards only after appropriate audit and review.

What could Node operators receive?

Any future rewards, if approved, should recognize a participant’s share of accepted eligible work during a given period. No fixed return is promised per GPU, CPU, hour or device.

01

Work score

The system measures accepted tasks, complexity, quality, duplication and defined anti-fraud criteria.

02

Share of a period

Individual weighted contribution divided by all eligible contribution — without creating rewards when no useful work exists.

03

Settlement and payout

Fees, operating reserve, thresholds and payout schedules will be published before the network opens.

FUTURE PRESALE / PROPOSAL

Future presale: only after solid foundations exist.

A possible presale to support research, GPUs, servers, development and security is under consideration. The illustrative 4% reserve is not an offer to buy.

Before any sale announcement, we would require validated legal and financial information, regulatory assessment, contracts and technical controls, clear risks, eligibility rules and a formal launch decision.

Questions and risks

Is there a new token available to buy?

No. This page describes a future proposal. There is no active contract, buy address, price or presale here.

Does a more powerful GPU guarantee higher earnings?

No. It may complete more useful work on certain tasks, but rewards depend on verified execution and future rules, with no guaranteed profitability.

Could a presale support Eureka?

That possibility is under review and will not be activated without legal assessment, transparency, documentation, security and a formal decision.

Are the network and terms final?

No. Supply, allocations, network, fees, issuance, eligibility and schedule are draft proposals subject to change.
