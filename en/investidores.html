Why are we building Eureka?

Our thesis is that the next generation of AI becomes more useful when it combines personal continuity, practical tools, user-controlled data and computing resources contributed by a network of participants.

01

Personal AI product

A Eureka with memory, digital personality, multimodality and practical real-life task support.

02

Proprietary infrastructure

Cognitive components, evaluation, memory and task integration under project control.

03

Compute Network

Potential to scale AI compute through distributed and verified work.

04

A future chat experience, tools, premium capabilities and computing services subject to validation.

05

Technical community

Collaboration with researchers, engineers, creators and hardware operators.

06

Transparency and measurement
