# EKNX — Planned DEX Migration

Status:

**PLANNED — NOT ACTIVE**

## Objective

The current project architecture intends to use the Launch Curve as the initial EKNX market mechanism and later transition market activity toward a decentralised exchange environment.

## Reserved allocation

From the 1,000,000 EKNX Market Reserve, the current intended framework preserves:

**350,000 EKNX**

for the future DEX/liquidity stage.

## Liquidity funding

The current planning model also intends to direct approximately:

**70% of Launch Curve proceeds**

toward liquidity / DEX market infrastructure.

This remains a planning parameter until the final launch documentation and on-chain configuration are approved.

## Migration conditions

DEX migration should only occur after predefined launch conditions have been met, including:

- Launch Curve technical validation
- Final liquidity calculation
- Treasury/liquidity accounting
- Smart-contract verification
- Security review
- Legal/regulatory review
- Final market disclosures
- Selection and validation of the DEX and trading pair
- Publication of official contract and pool addresses

## Price

Eureka Nexus does not guarantee:

- DEX listing price
- Market value
- Liquidity
- Appreciation
- Trading volume
- Investment return

Once decentralised trading exists, market value can be affected by supply, demand, liquidity and external market conditions.
