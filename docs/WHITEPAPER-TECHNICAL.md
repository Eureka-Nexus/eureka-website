# 1. Executive summary

Eureka Nexus is a Portuguese technology project developing an ecosystem
that combines artificial intelligence, personal digital identity,
distributed computation, mining infrastructure and EKNX.

The project is built around several connected layers:

1. Eureka AI
2. Eureka 2026 Miner
3. Eureka Nexus Mining Network
4. EKNX
5. Automatic mining payout infrastructure
6. Future Launch Curve
7. Future decentralised exchange liquidity
8. Future Eureka applications and agents

The project's objective is broader than launching a crypto-asset.

Eureka Nexus aims to build a persistent digital intelligence ecosystem
where each user can progressively shape their own Eureka.

EKNX is intended to operate as one economic and utility layer within
that broader ecosystem.

# 2. Project vision

## 2.1 Beyond Crypto. Beyond AI.

Eureka Nexus is based on the idea that digital intelligence should be
capable of developing continuity over time.

Instead of every user receiving an identical temporary chatbot,
the long-term Eureka architecture is intended to allow a user to shape
a persistent digital entity through:

- identity;
- appearance;
- personality;
- voice;
- memory;
- goals;
- permissions;
- knowledge;
- tools;
- experiences;
- specialised agents.

The project refers to this direction as a digital being or persistent
digital intelligence.

This terminology describes the product vision.

Eureka Nexus does not claim that the current system is conscious,
sentient or biologically alive.

## 3.2 Visual identity

The Eureka identity architecture is being designed around a persistent
canonical visual identity.

The intended workflow is:

1. User creates an Eureka profile
2. User describes the desired appearance
3. A temporary visual preview is generated
4. User may regenerate another preview
5. User confirms the preferred identity
6. The system preserves a canonical identity representation
7. A persistent reference is associated with that identity
8. Future Eureka applications may recover the same profile and visual
   identity

Production authentication and recovery mechanisms remain under
development.

A simple public reference code must not be treated as production-grade
authentication until the final recovery architecture is implemented.

# 50. Notification is not regulatory approval

Under the applicable MiCA Title II notification framework, competent
authorities do not provide prior approval of the crypto-asset white
paper merely through the notification process.

Eureka Nexus must therefore not describe a notified document as:

- Approved by MiCA
- Approved by the European Union
- Approved by ESMA
- Approved by a competent authority

unless a separate legal basis actually supports such a statement.

Regulatory notification and regulatory approval are different concepts.
