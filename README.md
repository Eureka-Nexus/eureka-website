# Eureka Nexus

**Beyond Crypto. Beyond AI.**

Eureka Nexus is a Portuguese technology project building an ecosystem around:

- Eureka AI
- Persistent personal digital identity
- AI agents and tools
- Eureka Nexus Mining Network
- Eureka 2026 Miner
- EKNX on BNB Smart Chain
- Automatic mining rewards
- Planned EKNX Launch Curve
- Planned transition to DEX liquidity

Official website:

https://eurekanexus.pt

## Eureka AI

Eureka AI is being developed as a persistent and personalised digital intelligence.

The long-term direction includes:

- User-defined identity and appearance
- Voice
- Personality
- Long-term memory
- Goals and planning
- Tools and agents
- Files and applications
- Research
- Coding
- Automation
- Permission-based visual context
- Persistent digital environments

Eureka Nexus does not claim that the current Eureka system is conscious or sentient.

## EKNX Market

Current status:

**COMING SOON — NOT ACTIVE**

The current planned framework is:

- 1,000,000 EKNX total Market Reserve
- Up to 650,000 EKNX intended for the Launch Curve
- 350,000 EKNX intended for the future DEX/liquidity stage
- Continuous progressive curve
- No artificial fixed price tiers
- Current pricing model:
  **price calculated live on-chain by the deployed Genesis bonding curve**

The final curve formula and on-chain parameters are not active yet.

Current planned use of Launch Curve proceeds:

- 70% — liquidity / future DEX structure
- 30% — Eureka Nexus development, infrastructure and operations
