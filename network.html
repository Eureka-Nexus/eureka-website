HOW IT WORKS

From validated work to EKNX entitlement.

1
ChallengeThe official server issues time-bounded CPU or GPU mining work.
2
VerificationSubmitted shares are checked by the Eureka PoW verifiers. Reported hashrate alone does not create rewards.
3
AccountingValid work contributes to the miner's reward allocation. The official pool fee is fixed at 1% and the miner net is 99%.
4
Settlement & claimCompleted mining accounting is designed around cumulative daily settlements and on-chain claims.
TRANSPARENCY

Public information, private keys stay private.

The website and public network endpoints should expose statistics and public addresses only. The Miner should never request or store a seed phrase or private key.
